Venue: Hard Rock Stadium
City: Miami Gardens, Florida (Miami metro)
Capacity: 65,326
Role: Group stage and knockout matches

Getting There: Metrorail to Palmetto station then rideshare or connecting bus. Express shuttles from downtown Miami and Miami Beach on match days. Rideshares widely available.

Fan Zone: Bayfront Park and Bayside Marketplace in downtown Miami host official fan zones. Miami's large Latin American community makes this the most vibrant atmosphere in the tournament for South American teams.

Local Tips: June in Miami is hot and humid with frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Bring rain gear. Wynwood Arts District is excellent for dining. Miami Beach accommodation is 30+ minutes from the stadium.

Nearby: Miami Beach, Art Deco District, Wynwood Walls, Little Havana, Everglades (1 hour).
