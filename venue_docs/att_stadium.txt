Venue: AT&T Stadium
City: Arlington, Texas (Dallas-Fort Worth metro)
Capacity: 80,000 (expandable to 100,000)
Role: Group stage and knockout matches

Getting There: No direct public transit to the stadium. Rideshare from Dallas or Fort Worth recommended. Official shuttles run from downtown Dallas and Fort Worth on match days. Parking available on-site.

Fan Zone: Klyde Warren Park and Dallas Fair Park in downtown Dallas host official fan activities.

Local Tips: June temperatures in Dallas can exceed 100°F (38°C). The stadium has a retractable roof and is fully air-conditioned. Stay hydrated outdoors. Deep Ellum neighborhood is best for pre-match dining and nightlife.

Nearby: Dallas Arts District, Sixth Floor Museum, Fort Worth Stockyards.
