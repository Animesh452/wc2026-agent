Venue: Estadio Azteca
City: Mexico City, Mexico
Capacity: 87,523
Role: Opening match and group stage matches

Getting There: Mexico City Metro Line 2 to Tasqueña, then Tren Ligero to the dedicated Estadio Azteca station. The stadium has its own metro stop — use public transit. Rideshares available but traffic is heavy.

Fan Zone: The Zócalo (city center plaza) and Chapultepec Park host major fan events for Mexico City matches.

Altitude Warning: Mexico City sits at 2,240 meters (7,349 feet) above sea level. Fans arriving from lower altitudes may experience breathlessness and fatigue. Stay hydrated, take it easy your first day, and avoid alcohol on arrival.

History: Azteca hosted the 1970 and 1986 World Cup Finals — the most iconic stadium in the tournament. Maradona's Hand of God and Goal of the Century both happened here in 1986.

Local Tips: June is the start of rainy season — bring a rain jacket. Roma and Condesa neighborhoods have excellent restaurants and nightlife.

Nearby: Zócalo historic center, Teotihuacán pyramids (1 hour), Frida Kahlo Museum, Chapultepec Castle.
