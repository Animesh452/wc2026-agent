Venue: Mercedes-Benz Stadium
City: Atlanta, Georgia
Capacity: 71,000
Role: Group stage and knockout matches

Getting There: MARTA rail (Blue or Green line) to Vine City or CNN Center station — best option. The stadium is steps from MARTA. Rideshares available throughout Atlanta.

Fan Zone: Centennial Olympic Park in downtown Atlanta hosts fan activities and screenings.

Local Tips: Atlanta summers are hot and humid but the stadium is fully climate-controlled with a retractable roof. Known for affordable stadium concessions. Ponce City Market and the Atlanta BeltLine are excellent for pre-match activities.

Nearby: Georgia Aquarium (world's largest), World of Coca-Cola, National Center for Civil and Human Rights, Atlanta BeltLine trail.
