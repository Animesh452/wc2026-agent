Venue: MetLife Stadium
City: East Rutherford, New Jersey (New York metro area)
Capacity: 82,500
Role: Final match venue for World Cup 2026

Getting There: NJ Transit train from Penn Station takes 30 minutes via the Meadowlands Rail Line directly to the stadium. Rideshares available from Manhattan. Public transit strongly recommended over driving.

Fan Zone: Times Square and Brooklyn waterfront host official FIFA Fan Zones with free entry, giant screens, and entertainment.

Local Tips: Book accommodation in New York City proper. June weather is warm and humid. The stadium is the largest in the tournament. Manhattan is 20 minutes away with world-class dining, Broadway, and nightlife.

Nearby: Central Park, the High Line, Statue of Liberty, Brooklyn Bridge.
