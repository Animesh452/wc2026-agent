Venue: SoFi Stadium
City: Inglewood, California (Los Angeles area)
Capacity: 70,240
Role: Group stage and knockout matches

Getting There: LA Metro C Line (Green Line) to Hawthorne/Lennox station then shuttle to stadium. Rideshares available throughout LA. Heavy traffic expected — allow extra time. Public transit strongly recommended.

Fan Zone: LA Live in downtown Los Angeles hosts the official fan zone with concerts, screenings, and activities.

Local Tips: June weather is pleasant at 70-80°F with low humidity. Traffic in LA is notoriously heavy — budget 90 minutes from Hollywood or Santa Monica. The stadium has a translucent roof providing shade.

Nearby: Santa Monica beach (30 min), Hollywood (25 min), Venice Beach, Universal Studios.
