Venue: Levi's Stadium
City: Santa Clara, California (San Francisco Bay Area)
Capacity: 68,500
Role: Group stage and knockout matches

Getting There: VTA Light Rail to Great America station (walking distance). Caltrain to Santa Clara station with connecting shuttle. Rideshares from San Jose (20 min) and San Francisco (45-60 min).

Fan Zone: San Francisco Civic Center Plaza hosts the official fan zone for Bay Area matches.

Local Tips: Bay Area summers are cool compared to other host cities. Bring a light jacket — evening temperatures can drop to 55°F (13°C). San Francisco is 45 minutes away and worth visiting. Napa Valley is 1 hour north.

Nearby: San Francisco (Golden Gate Bridge, Fisherman's Wharf), Silicon Valley, Napa Valley wine country.
