Venue: BC Place
City: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Capacity: 54,500
Role: Group stage matches

Getting There: Vancouver SkyTrain to Stadium-Chinatown station — the stadium is steps away. Excellent public transit from throughout Vancouver. Rideshares available.

Fan Zone: Canada Place waterfront and the surrounding downtown area host fan activities with mountain backdrop views.

Local Tips: Vancouver in June is one of the most pleasant World Cup venues — temperatures of 65-72°F (18-22°C) with low humidity. Extremely walkable downtown. Gastown and Yaletown neighborhoods have excellent dining and bars.

Nearby: Stanley Park seawall, Granville Island Public Market, Capilano Suspension Bridge, Whistler ski resort (1.5 hours north), Victoria via ferry.
