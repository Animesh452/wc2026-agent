FIFA World Cup 2026 Overview

World Cup 2026 is the first tournament to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. It is co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico — the first World Cup hosted by three nations.

Tournament Dates: June 11 to July 19, 2026

Format:
- Group Stage: 48 teams in 12 groups of 4
- Round of 32: Top 2 from each group plus 8 best third-place teams advance
- Then Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, Third place match, and Final
- Total: 104 matches

Host Cities and Venues:
United States (11 venues): New York/New Jersey (MetLife), Dallas (AT&T Stadium), Los Angeles (SoFi Stadium), San Francisco Bay Area (Levi's Stadium), Miami (Hard Rock Stadium), Atlanta (Mercedes-Benz Stadium), Kansas City (Arrowhead Stadium), Boston (Gillette Stadium), Philadelphia (Lincoln Financial Field), Houston (NRG Stadium), Seattle (Lumen Field)
Canada (2 venues): Vancouver (BC Place), Toronto (BMO Field)
Mexico (3 venues): Mexico City (Estadio Azteca), Monterrey (Estadio BBVA), Guadalajara (Estadio Akron)

Opening Match: June 11, 2026 at Estadio Azteca, Mexico City
Final: July 19, 2026 at MetLife Stadium, New York/New Jersey

Fan Zone Info: Every host city has a free official FIFA Fan Zone with giant screens, entertainment, and food. No ticket required for fan zones.

Travel Tips: Domestic flights connect US host cities. No single city has all matches — fans following a team will likely travel between cities. Book accommodation and flights early as prices surge around match days.
